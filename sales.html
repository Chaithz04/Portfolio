Project Overview

The Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze overall business sales performance.


The dashboard transforms raw sales transaction data into meaningful insights by analyzing monthly sales trends, product performance, salesperson contribution, district-wise sales and customer payment behaviour.


The objective of this project was to present key sales information through a clear and visually interactive dashboard that supports faster business analysis and decision-making.

Project Objective

  • Analyze overall sales performance and monthly sales trends.
  • Identify top-performing products and product categories.
  • Compare sales contribution across salespersons.
  • Evaluate high-value and normal orders by salesperson.
  • Analyze district-wise sales performance.
  • Understand customer payment mode preferences.

Dashboard Analysis

📈 Monthly Sales Trend

Sales increased from January and reached the highest level in March. A decline is visible during April and May, followed by a slight recovery in June.


📦 Product Performance

Cardamom 100g generated the highest sales revenue at ₹34,650, making it the strongest revenue-contributing product. However, Coconut Oil 1L recorded the highest quantity sold, showing that the highest-selling product by quantity is not necessarily the highest revenue contributor.


👤 Salesperson Performance

Vishnu generated the highest overall sales among all salespersons. The high-value versus normal order analysis also shows that Vishnu recorded the highest number of high-value orders, indicating a strong contribution from larger-value transactions.


📍 District-wise Performance

Ernakulam recorded the highest sales at ₹33,270, followed by Kozhikode. Thrissur generated the lowest sales among the districts analyzed, highlighting a potential area for further sales improvement.


💳 Payment Behaviour

Cash was the most frequently used payment mode with 23 orders, followed by Card with 21 orders and UPI with 16 orders. Although digital payment options are actively used, cash remains the leading payment preference in the dataset.

Key Insights

  • March recorded the highest monthly sales, followed by a noticeable decline through April and May.
  • Cardamom 100g was the highest revenue-generating product with ₹34,650 in sales.
  • Coconut Oil 1L recorded the highest quantity sold at 78 units.
  • Vishnu was the top-performing salesperson and also recorded the highest number of high-value orders.
  • Ernakulam was the strongest sales district with ₹33,270 in sales.
  • Cash was the most frequently used payment mode, accounting for 23 orders.
  • The difference between revenue leaders and quantity leaders shows the importance of analyzing both sales value and sales volume.

Business Value

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance and helps identify key trends across products, salespersons, districts and payment modes.


The insights can support businesses in identifying high-performing products, evaluating salesperson performance and recognizing underperforming sales regions.


By comparing sales revenue with quantity sold, the dashboard also helps distinguish between products that generate higher revenue and products that achieve higher sales volume.
